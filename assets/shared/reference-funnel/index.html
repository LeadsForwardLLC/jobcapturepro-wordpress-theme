QUESTIONS CONTRACTORS ACTUALLY ASK

Clear answers before you start.

Does my crew have to learn another app?

No. Crews can capture a job in the JobCapturePro app, or job activity can flow in through supported integrations. If your current workflow already captures the right information, the goal is to preserve it — not add busywork.

I already use CompanyCam or Housecall Pro. Why would I need JCP?

Those tools are useful for documentation and operations. JobCapturePro is focused on turning real job activity into public-facing proof: website content, Google activity, social content, directory presence and review opportunities.

Does JobCapturePro guarantee Google rankings?

No. No legitimate platform can guarantee a Google ranking. Consistent job activity can support local relevance and keep your profile fresh, but results depend on many factors.

What happens after I start the 14-day trial?

You choose your capture workflow, connect the channels you want to use and publish your first real proof. The goal of the trial is activation — not wandering around a dashboard.

What gets published from one completed job?

Depending on your setup, one completed job can support your website, Google Business Profile, social content, JobCapturePro directory presence and an on-site review opportunity via QR code or link.
